# A trip with a year carries an itinerary; Meridian hands off real booking and never takes money

Extends ADR 0007; changes nothing for trips without a year.

ADR 0007 left nights per stop and exact dates "to the booking step". That step now exists, on the Trip itself. Once a trip has a `year`, each stop can carry `arrive` and `depart` dates (nights are derived, never stored), a stay (`lodging`: name, type, address, check-in and out, price, booking reference, link), `activities` and `notes`. A trip with no year is still a season and shows none of it; the months stay on the stop either way, and dates outside them are a warning, not an error. Validation separates contradictions (leaving before arriving, overlapping stops, check-out before check-in, a recorded arrival that is not after its departure once both ends' time zones are applied) from probable slips (an activity outside the stop). The editor reads the result day by day, folding quiet days in one place into a single row. The shapes mirror the journal's Lodging and Experience so a trip can later be promoted into a Journey, but costs are kept exactly as entered (amount and currency) and are never converted.

Legs have a mode: flight, train, bus, ferry or drive. A leg's plan (`mode`, and a booking recorded by hand) is stored on the stop it arrives at, with `Trip.homeLeg` for the way back, stamped with the city it leaves from; reorder the route and the stamp no longer matches, so the stale plan is ignored instead of describing the wrong journey. The default mode comes from distance and geography: `data/city-geo.json` gives every city a landmass group (Natural Earth 1:10m land, polygons joined when their coasts come within 5 km, plus five named fixed links such as the Channel Tunnel). Same landmass means road up to 100 km, bus to 250, train to 700, then a flight; across the sea means a ferry up to 300 km, then a flight. It is a starting point, not knowledge of what runs there, and the traveller can change it. Ground and sea estimates are stated assumptions (straight-line distance × 1.25 for the route, a speed and a rate per km per mode), shown next to the numbers. Only flight legs between two airports reach flight search. The map draws non-flight legs dotted.

Meridian never moves real money. Flights inside the app remain Duffel test mode or mock, and every surface now says so: the Flights page and the booking sheet carry a "Test mode" banner, the sheet is titled "Test booking", and a test booking on a trip is a warning badge that does not count as booked. Real booking is a hand-off: "Book on…" opens Google Flights, Skyscanner or Kayak (Rome2Rio and Google Maps for ground legs) and Booking.com, Airbnb or Flatio for stays, with whatever that site's URL can carry prefilled, and each link says what that is. Only airport codes, city names, dates and a head count go into those URLs. Afterwards the traveller records the booking on the leg (carrier, number, reference, price, local times) and the leg shows as booked.

Costs are honest about their basis. Entered prices are summed per currency. Transport without a recorded price gets the labelled estimate. A stay without a price is estimated only from the owner's own journal (the median nightly cost of past stays in that city, or of two or more in that country); otherwise it reads "not estimated". The map's synthesized cost layer is not used.

Calendar: the trip exports as an `.ics` file built in the browser, and as a feed at `/api/shared/calendar/<token>.ics`. Stays are all-day spans over the nights; legs and activities are timed where a time is known. Times are typed as local wall-clock times and written as UTC instants using each city's IANA zone (also in `data/city-geo.json`, from `@photostructure/tz-lookup`, a build-time dependency only); without a zone they are written as floating times and say so. Sharing: `/api/shared/trip/<token>` serves a server-rendered, script-free, read-only page. Both links are 192-bit random tokens in a `tripLinks.json` document, one per trip and kind, replaced on re-creation and revocable; they are created behind `MERIDIAN_TOKEN` and opened without it, because calendar apps and the people a trip is shared with cannot send it. The shared page is built from a stripped copy of the trip, and the feed with `includePrivate: false`: neither carries booking references, prices, links, notes, the traveller count or anything from Bookings or the journal. The downloaded file is the owner's own and includes references.

Rejected: live payments or a production Duffel key (real money must not move from a personal tool with no auth on `/api/bookings`); storing nights instead of dates (two sources of truth); keying leg plans by leg index (a reorder would silently move a booking onto another leg); a time-zone library in the bundle (the zone is data on the city; Intl does the arithmetic); Omio links (no stable URL that prefills a route); a per-city accommodation price table (no real source in the repo); a client-side route for shared trips (it would ship the whole app, and its token, to a visitor); and hashing link tokens at rest (the owner needs to copy the link again, and the store already holds the trip itself).
