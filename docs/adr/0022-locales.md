# Locales are researched places to live, kept as data, with their own calendar

Builds on ADR 0011 (Rotation, Personal Windows, Fit) and ADR 0017 (the Notion bridge). Leaves both as they are.

The owner researches where to live before any stay exists: a scored list of bases, hubs and one-off destinations with the months to be there, how long a place holds up, and how to get in. A Rotation Stay is a commitment with dates; a Place Profile is the owner's own months for one city; the Notion pool (ADR 0017) is candidates for the solver. None of them can say "Da Nang / Hoi An is a base, best February to May, two to three months, on a 90-day e-visa". So a **Locale** is its own primitive.

**Model** (`src/lib/locales.ts`, pure; data in `data/locales.json`). A locale has an id, a name, a country, a region, a role (`base`, `hub` or `destination`) and one or more City ids, the first being the anchor whose Comfort Index and place data stand for the locale. Twelve researched month ratings (`best`, `good`, `fair`, `avoid`) with short reasons, a stay range in weeks (minimum, ideal, maximum, with why), an all-in cost range as written (EUR per month, or per trip for a destination), the owner's own 1–10 scores where the research has them, an asymmetry rank, the long-stay entry route with a source link and the month it was checked, timing-worthy events, the airport, an optional booking lead time, and corrections where the review found the owner's notes wrong. Where the place has no city in the dataset (Kotor, Saranda, Chania, Polignano), the nearest one stands in and `area` says so.

**Months.** Each month reads the owner's Personal Window for the anchor city first (ADR 0011: still the owner's word), then the locale's rating, then the Comfort Index. The Comfort Index is always shown beside the month, and a month where it reads two or more fit levels away is marked rather than overridden: the index knows temperature, rain and air, but not typhoons, crowds or burning season.

**Ideal staying windows** (`stayWindows`). A window is a run of great or good months, wrapping the new year, or of fair months when there is none (marked). Its weeks are the locale's stay range cut to the run's length and, while signed in, to the day limit of the owner's best passport for that country (`visa/data.cellOf`; free movement and the home country have none). The stay itself is the ideal length in whole months, placed on the stretch of the run with the most great months. Windows are ranked by great months, then run length. Rolling limits such as Schengen 90/180 are the Days page's job and are only pointed to.

**Page** (`#/locales`, public; the data is research, not personal). A rolling twelve-month calendar from this month: one row per locale, each cell coloured with the rotation's fit ramp, a bar under the ideal stay, a thin line under the rest of the good run, dots for events, and marks for comfort disagreement and the owner's own windows. Rows group by region, or sort by the owner's score, by the mean of Meridian's metric surfaces (quality, safety, healthcare, affordability, internet), or by fit in a chosen month (click a month heading). Below it, the top three bases for each month. A sheet per locale shows every month with its reason and Comfort Index, the windows, the owner's scores beside Meridian's metrics, entry, corrections and events. While signed in, "Add to rotation" writes an idea stay for the next time the window comes round through the rotation's existing stay API, with the window's nights, the research's upper cost as budget and the locale's lead time; ideas never count as commitments (ADR 0011).

**Where the data came from.** The owner's Notion page "Research on Locations" (scores and sources there: Numbeo 2026, CEOWORLD 2025, OAG 2025, TasteAtlas 25/26, EIU, Global Peace Index), reviewed in November 2026 for monsoon, typhoon, burning-season and air-quality timing and for current entry rules. The month ratings are authored data with reasons, not parsed prose, which keeps ADR 0017's rule: the Notion importer still does not turn "Best:" and "Avoid:" lines into windows. Corrections are kept on the locale, not silently applied to the owner's notes. Entry rules change; each carries its checked month, and Settings › Visas remains the source for day counting.

Rejected: Locales as Place Profiles (a profile is per city and the owner's own; locales span cities and are research the owner may disagree with); as solver pool entries (the pool is the solver's input; a locale can feed it later through `stayFromLocale`); storing them server-side per owner (shared research with no edits yet, shipped like the other datasets; a per-owner override belongs in Personal Windows); and letting the Comfort Index override the research (it cannot see typhoons or smoke seasons).
