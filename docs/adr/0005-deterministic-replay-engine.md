# Replay is a deterministic engine; the LLM only calls tools

Fingerprinting and similarity ranking are pure functions with unit tests (fixture weather for stability). The agent's `search_journal`, `get_fingerprint`, `find_similar_windows` and `draft_journey` tools call them, so the LLM chooses arguments and narrates results but never scores or ranks, and Replay works fully with no LLM key. Granularity is monthly (D5): cells are destination × month Windows, matching the monthly seasonality dataset, and weekly is not interpolated. A dimension that is absent or pending for a pair is dropped from that pair's similarity and the weights are renormalized; each result reports `scoredOn` and the universe size. Crowd is always a labeled proxy (the city's comfort-peak curve plus holiday overlap, overridable per stop), never presented as measured. The synthesized surfaces `scoring.ts` (economics, quality, stability) and `datasets.ts` (tourism, cost) are excluded as signals: they are deterministic hash output (`source: "synthesized"`), and feeding them in would produce confident nonsense. Mock-sourced prices carry the mock badge and are excluded from the spend dimension. Rejected: letting the model rank results from its own judgment (not reproducible, not testable, and the mock path would diverge from it), and interpolating monthly normals into weeks (fabricated resolution).
