# Trip plans

Four seasonal trips. Each is a route through the months it works best in, and you can take it any year.
`trips.json` loads them into Meridian: **Settings › Trips › Import**, then pick the file.
Re-importing updates them in place (same ids). With the journal unlocked, they sync to every device.

Every stop carries its months, so Meridian shows the trip's season, each stop's comfort in its months,
and the events that happen while you're there. Choosing a year in the trip's **Season** card turns the months
into dates: flight searches start on them, and that year's moving holidays (Tết, Lunar New Year) show up.

The dates below are one worked example of each season, to make nights per stop concrete.
Comfort scores come from `data/seasonality.json` (2015–2024 climate data).

| Trip | Season | Length | Who | First year it fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Southeast Asia, dry season into monsoon | February to July | ~150 nights | solo | 2027 |
| East Africa family safari | July to August | 14 nights | family of 3 | 2028 (lodges book ~a year out) |
| South America overland | September to October | 30 nights | open | 2027 |
| Japan in autumn colours | October to November | ~54 nights | open | 2027 |

## 1. Southeast Asia, dry season into monsoon (February to July)

One long, slow trip across both seasons: the dry season through Vietnam and the coasts,
then the start of the monsoon in places that are greener and emptier in the rain.
Each stop is timed for its own season, because the monsoon doesn't reach every coast at the same time.

Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Hanoi → Ninh Bình → Hue → Da Nang → Da Lat → Ho Chi Minh City → Phuket →
George Town → Kuala Lumpur → Yogyakarta → Bali → Siem Reap → Phnom Penh → Luang Prabang → Chiang Mai → Bangkok

### Phase 1: dry season (Feb – Apr)

| Stop | Dates (approx.) | Nights | Why then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bangkok | 1–5 Feb | 4 | Arrival; comfort index 88 in Feb |
| Chiang Mai | 5–19 Feb | 14 | Cool and clear. Leave before the burning-season smoke (Mar–Apr) |
| Hanoi | 19–25 Feb | 6 | After Tết in most years, so the city has reopened; index 97. Tết moves (6 Feb 2027, 26 Jan 2028, 13 Feb 2029): in a late year, shift Vietnam a week later. Meridian flags the clash once a year is chosen |
| Ninh Bình | 25–28 Feb | 3 | Karst rivers and rice paddies |
| Hue | 28 Feb – 3 Mar | 3 | Imperial city; central coast drying out |
| Da Nang | 3–15 Mar | 12 | Base for Hoi An. March is the sweet spot before the heat |
| Da Lat | 15–20 Mar | 5 | Highlands, cool break |
| Ho Chi Minh City | 20–25 Mar | 5 | Mekong Delta trips |
| Phuket | 25 Mar – 8 Apr | 14 | Last of the Andaman dry season; Phang Nga Bay, Phi Phi |
| George Town | 8–16 Apr | 8 | Food capital; avoids Songkran (13–15 Apr) chaos in Thailand, or go back for it |
| Kuala Lumpur | 16–20 Apr | 4 | Hub for the hop to Java |

### Phase 2: as the monsoon arrives (late Apr – Jul)

Indonesia's dry season is the mainland's wet one, so May there is dry and still quiet before the July–August peak.
From June the mainland turns green, prices drop and the crowds thin out. Rain mostly comes as afternoon downpours.

| Stop | Dates (approx.) | Nights | Why then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yogyakarta | 20–28 Apr | 8 | Borobudur and Prambanan at the start of Java's dry season |
| Bali | 28 Apr – 26 May | 28 | Dry, between the Easter and summer crowds. Ubud, Sidemen, east coast |
| Siem Reap | 26 May – 5 Jun | 10 | Green season: Angkor nearly empty, moats full, jungle lush |
| Phnom Penh | 5–9 Jun | 4 | On the way north |
| Luang Prabang | 9–21 Jun | 12 | Kuang Si falls at full flow, quiet town, rice planting |
| Chiang Mai | 21 Jun – 1 Jul | 10 | Same city as February, but green and empty. Good for a slow finish |
| Bangkok | 1–3 Jul | 2 | Fly out |

Luang Prabang shows up as "Louangphrabang" in Meridian's city list.

### Before booking

- **Visas over long stays:** 36 nights in Indonesia is more than a standard 30-day visa on arrival, so plan an extension or a different visa. Also check Thailand's current visa-exempt length (it has changed often) and Vietnam's 90-day e-visa against your passport.
- **Northern Thailand smoke:** March–April burning season is why Chiang Mai comes early and then again in June.
- **Monsoon days:** pack for heavy afternoon rain. Some boat trips (Ha Long, Andaman islands) run less or stop in the wet season, which is why the coasts come in Phase 1.
- **Same year as the safari:** this season ends in early July and the safari starts mid-July, so taking both in one year leaves about two weeks between them.

## 2. East Africa family safari (July to August)

Nairobi → (Masai Mara) → Arusha → Zanzibar

| Stop | Nights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Nairobi | 2 | Arrival; Giraffe Centre and the Sheldrick elephant orphanage (good with a child) |
| Masai Mara | 4 | Fly-in from Wilson Airport; Great Migration river crossings |
| Arusha | 4 | Base for Tarangire and Ngorongoro Crater |
| Zanzibar | 4 | Beach and Stone Town to finish |

The Mara has no city in Meridian's dataset, so it isn't a node: book it as Nairobi (Wilson) → Mara airstrip → Kilimanjaro (JRO) bush flights.

Why these dates: dry season, the migration is usually in the Mara Jul–Sep, and it lines up with northern-hemisphere school holidays.
Comfort index Nairobi 98 (Jul) / 100 (Aug).

Before booking:
- Peak-season family lodges sell out. Book 10–12 months ahead, so around September of the year before.
- Check minimum ages: some camps, walking safaris and private conservancies set 6+ or 12+.
- Malaria: see a travel clinic about prophylaxis for all three, including the child's dose.
- Kenya needs an eTA; Tanzania a visa/e-visa. Check yellow fever certificate rules for the Kenya → Tanzania crossing.

## 3. South America overland (September to October)

Lima → Cusco → La Paz → (Uyuni) → Calama → Salta → Mendoza → Buenos Aires → Foz do Iguaçu → Rio de Janeiro

| Stop | Nights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Lima | 2 | Arrival, food, Miraflores / Barranco |
| Cusco | 5 | Acclimatize; Sacred Valley; Machu Picchu |
| La Paz | 2 | Cable cars, Witches' Market |
| Uyuni → San Pedro de Atacama | 6 | 3-day jeep over the salt flat to Chile, then 3 nights in Atacama |
| Salta | 3 | Quebrada de Humahuaca, Cafayate wine |
| Mendoza | 3 | Malbec, Andes views |
| Buenos Aires | 4 | Neighbourhoods, tango, asado |
| Foz do Iguaçu | 2 | Both sides of the falls |
| Rio de Janeiro | 3 | Sugarloaf, Christ the Redeemer, beaches |

Uyuni and San Pedro aren't in Meridian's dataset. The La Paz → Calama leg stands in for the
overland salt-flat crossing (fly La Paz → Uyuni, jeep to San Pedro), and Calama → Salta is a bus over the Andes.
Don't book flights on those two legs.

Why these dates: end of the Andean dry season (Cusco, Altiplano), spring in Argentina.
Comfort index Lima 94 → 97, Santiago (proxy for Mendoza) 79 → 89, Buenos Aires 79 → 87 (Sep → Oct).

Before booking:
- Machu Picchu tickets and the Inca Trail (if wanted) sell out months ahead.
- Altitude: Cusco 3,400 m, La Paz 3,600 m, Uyuni crossing up to ~4,900 m. Go up gradually, as routed.

## 4. Japan in autumn colours (October to November)

Sapporo → Matsumoto → Kanazawa → Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka → Hiroshima → Fukuoka

Why autumn: October is the best month in Meridian's comfort index across most of the route
(Fukuoka 91, Hiroshima 87, Tokyo 76, Kyoto 75), and the colours move from Hokkaido and the mountains
in early October down to Kyoto and Miyajima in late November, so the route follows them.
It is quieter than cherry-blossom season and misses Golden Week (29 Apr – 5 May).
The other good window is mid-May to early June (Tokyo 83 in May), after Golden Week and before the rainy season.

| Stop | Dates (example) | Nights | Why then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sapporo | 5–11 Oct | 6 | Hokkaido colours peak first (Daisetsuzan, Jozankei). Leave before it turns cold (comfort 58 in Oct, 28 in Nov) |
| Matsumoto | 11–17 Oct | 6 | Japan Alps: Kamikochi (closes mid-November), Norikura, Takayama and Shirakawa-go as side trips |
| Kanazawa | 17–21 Oct | 4 | Kenroku-en, the old districts; comfort 73 in October |
| Tokyo | 21 Oct – 5 Nov | 15 | City, plus Nikko (colours late October) and Hakone (early November) as day or overnight trips |
| Kyoto | 5–19 Nov | 14 | Temples as the colours come in; Nara as a day trip. Peak is mid to late November |
| Osaka | 19–22 Nov | 3 | Food; Minoo falls for maples |
| Hiroshima | 22–25 Nov | 3 | Peace Park; Miyajima's Momijidani at its peak |
| Fukuoka | 25–28 Nov | 3 | Best comfort of the trip (91 in October, 73 in November); fly out |

Osaka appears as "Ōsaka" (`saka-japan` in Meridian's city list).

Before booking:
- **Kyoto in November** is its second-busiest season: book rooms months ahead, go to the famous temples at opening time, and use quieter areas (Ohara, Kurama, Takao) for colours.
- **Long weekends fill trains and hotels:** Sports Day (second Monday of October), Culture Day (3 November), Labour Thanksgiving Day (23 November). Check which fall on a Monday in your year.
- **Typhoons** are possible into early October, mostly in the south; the route starts in the north for that reason too.
- **Rail:** the national JR Pass got much more expensive in 2023. Compare it with regional passes and single tickets for this route before buying.
