# Trip plans 2027–2029

Three trips, one per year. `trips-2027-2029.json` loads them into Meridian:
**Settings › Trips › Import**, then pick the file. Re-importing updates them in place (same ids).

Meridian trip nodes are cities without dates, so dates, nights and notes live here.
Comfort scores quoted below come from `data/seasonality.json` (2015–2024 climate normals).

## 1. Southeast Asia — Nov–Dec 2027

Bangkok → Chiang Mai → Hanoi → Da Nang → Ho Chi Minh City → Siem Reap → Phuket

| Stop | Nights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Bangkok | 4 | Arrival, temples, food, day trip to Ayutthaya |
| Chiang Mai | 4 | Old city, Doi Suthep, ethical elephant sanctuary, cooking class |
| Hanoi | 5 | Old Quarter; 1 night on Ha Long or Lan Ha Bay |
| Da Nang | 4 | Hoi An (30 min away), Marble Mountains, Hai Van Pass |
| Ho Chi Minh City | 3 | War Remnants Museum, Cu Chi tunnels, Mekong Delta day |
| Siem Reap | 3 | Angkor temples (3-day pass) |
| Phuket | 5 | Beach finish; boat trips to Phang Nga Bay and Phi Phi |

Why these dates: dry season on the mainland. Comfort index Bangkok 83 (Nov) / 96 (Dec), Hanoi 95 / 98.
It also stays clear of Tết (Vietnamese New Year, early Feb), when much of Vietnam shuts.
The Andaman coast (Phuket) dries out from December, which is why it comes last.

Weak spot: central Vietnam (Da Nang, Hoi An) is still in its rainy season in late Nov / early Dec.
If the forecast looks bad, cut Da Nang to 2 nights and give them to Chiang Mai or Phuket.

## 2. East Africa family safari — Jul–Aug 2028

Nairobi → (Masai Mara) → Arusha → Zanzibar

| Stop | Nights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Nairobi | 2 | Arrival; Giraffe Centre and the Sheldrick elephant orphanage (good with a child) |
| Masai Mara | 4 | Fly-in from Wilson Airport; Great Migration river crossings |
| Arusha | 4 | Base for Tarangire and Ngorongoro Crater |
| Zanzibar | 4 | Beach and Stone Town to finish |

The Mara has no city in Meridian's dataset, so it isn't a node: book it as Nairobi (Wilson) → Mara airstrip → Kilimanjaro (JRO) bush flights.

Why these dates: dry season, the migration is usually in the Mara Jul–Sep, and it lines up with northern-hemisphere school holidays.
Comfort index Nairobi 98 (Jul) / 100 (Aug).

Before booking:
- Peak-season family lodges sell out. Book by ~Sep 2027.
- Check minimum ages: some camps, walking safaris and private conservancies set 6+ or 12+.
- Malaria: see a travel clinic about prophylaxis for all three, including the child's dose.
- Kenya needs an eTA; Tanzania a visa/e-visa. Check yellow fever certificate rules for the Kenya → Tanzania crossing.

## 3. South America — Sep–Oct 2029

Lima → Cusco → La Paz → (Uyuni) → Calama → Salta → Mendoza → Buenos Aires → Foz do Iguaçu → Rio de Janeiro

| Stop | Nights | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Lima | 2 | Arrival, food, Miraflores / Barranco |
| Cusco | 5 | Acclimatize; Sacred Valley; Machu Picchu |
| La Paz | 2 | Cable cars, Witches' Market |
| Uyuni → San Pedro de Atacama | 6 | 3-day jeep over the salt flat to Chile, then 3 nights in Atacama |
| Salta | 3 | Quebrada de Humahuaca, Cafayate wine |
| Mendoza | 3 | Malbec, Andes views |
| Buenos Aires | 4 | Neighbourhoods, tango, asado |
| Foz do Iguaçu | 2 | Both sides of the falls |
| Rio de Janeiro | 3 | Sugarloaf, Christ the Redeemer, beaches |

Uyuni and San Pedro aren't in Meridian's dataset. The La Paz → Calama leg stands in for the
overland salt-flat crossing (fly La Paz → Uyuni, jeep to San Pedro), and Calama → Salta is a bus over the Andes.
Don't book flights on those two legs.

Why these dates: end of the Andean dry season (Cusco, Altiplano), spring in Argentina.
Comfort index Lima 94 → 97, Santiago (proxy for Mendoza) 79 → 89, Buenos Aires 79 → 87 (Sep → Oct).

Before booking:
- Machu Picchu tickets and the Inca Trail (if wanted) sell out months ahead.
- Altitude: Cusco 3,400 m, La Paz 3,600 m, Uyuni crossing up to ~4,900 m. Go up gradually, as routed.
